M57 0.6.0 introduces the new native administrator space: Overview, searchable
Controls and Settings, with a scroll-aware dock and resumable setup.

- Strong passphrases and verified legacy-PIN migration, escalating failed-login
  waits, pause presets, custom expiry and indefinite pause.
- Editable AI app/domain rules, grouped experimental Meta controls and
  source-app model suggestions for new Lyra conversations. The companion Lyra
  handoff endpoint must be deployed for model suggestions to apply.
- Full-screen interventions, smooth countdown and separate short-video controls
  with administrator-selected blocking or reminders. New app coverage is experimental.
- Independent family hosts categories, validated HTTPS updates with fallbacks,
  allow/block exceptions, browser guards and an opt-in local UDP DNS VPN.
- Native battery access and extra manufacturer troubleshooting for Samsung,
  Xiaomi, Oppo, Vivo, Realme and OnePlus; narrow accessibility-settings guard.
- AGPLv3 original work, preserved Curbox GPLv3, full StevenBlack MIT credit,
  contributing-source terms and corresponding source for each signed APK.

Account recovery is excluded. Personal-device protection can be revoked through
Android; DNS filtering has Private/Secure DNS, cached/direct-IP and TCP limits.
The Samsung M13 / One UI 6 device checklist remains required. These builds are
published as prereleases until device acceptance is recorded. Read DEVICE_TESTS.md
and docs/releases.md before deployment. Do not uninstall an existing protected
installation simply to change signing keys; retain its key and configuration.
